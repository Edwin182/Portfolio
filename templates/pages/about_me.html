The experience behind the code.

I'm Edwin Gaitan, an EDI Business Systems Analyst based in Southern California. For more than eight years, I've worked with the data exchanges that help businesses process orders, coordinate shipments, and manage invoices.

Working with IBM Sterling B2B Integrator has taught me to investigate problems carefully, understand requirements, and test how systems behave together. A successful technical change needs to support the business process around it.

I'm now applying that approach to full-stack development. My projects give me practical experience with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Python, Django, and APIs while I continue developing my skills.

My service as a U.S. Navy Reserve Chief Petty Officer also shapes how I work: take responsibility, communicate clearly, and help the team move forward.
